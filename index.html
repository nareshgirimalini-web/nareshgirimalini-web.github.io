Pharmacy fraud prevention

A rule engine plus ML anomaly detection, built for a PBM and its plan sponsors.

Problem

Fraudulent claims and pharmacies were costing plan sponsors real money. Fixed rules catch known patterns but miss behavior nobody has written a rule for yet.

What I did

Designed it as two layers: deterministic rules for known patterns and policy, and ML anomaly detection for what rules can't anticipate. Designed the Phase 1 interface and fraud rules myself (fraud arrest and pharmacy suspension). The product is built and ready to launch. Phase 2 adds gradient-based pattern identification and ML models.

$2M–$4Mprojected first-year savings, about a 15% cut in fraudulent claims. Other clients are already asking about it.

Benefit Serve: plan certification

Configure a benefit plan, run test claims against it, and prove it's right before go-live, at one of the largest PBMs in the US.

Problem

Every plan has to be configured in the adjudication system and validated before it goes live. Across 100,000+ plans that testing was manual and slow.

What I did

Led the team that built Benefit Serve: configure a plan in the legacy adjudication system, automatically run test claims against it, and build test beds to validate the setup.

−25%time to market for new plans. It also played a major part in winning a $5B pharmacy benefits contract from a competitor.
